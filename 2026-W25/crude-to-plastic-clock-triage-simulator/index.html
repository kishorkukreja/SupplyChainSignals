Scenario notes

    Watch indicators

    Drewry/Freightos route-rate divergence July tariff or forced-labor comment windows Warehouse market vacancy / overflow quote Customer penalty clock vs promotion date

    Operator guide

    1. Name the clock. Tariff deadline, fuel surcharge, promotion date, production restart, customer penalty, or stockout risk. If no clock exists, the premium is probably anxiety.
    2. Separate rate indexes. Do not blend Drewry WCI, Freightos, NRF/Hackett, Port of LA, and ISM into a fake master index. They measure different things.
    3. Check the receiving system. Pull-forward only works if warehouse space, labor, compliance evidence, and SKU-level product data are ready.
    4. Use crude-to-plastic as the lens. Packaging, petrochemical exposure, bunker fuel, and consumer-goods timing sit on the same cost clock, but each decision still needs SKU economics.
    5. Prefer option value when uncertain. Split shipments or redesign packaging when demand confidence is medium/low and policy clocks are not yet binding.
