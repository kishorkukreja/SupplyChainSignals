### Domain notes

Primary/official documents FIRST and as the tie-breaker: PIB press releases, CBIC customs tariff notifications (notification number + effective date), RBI reference-rate archive, ICE Brent settlement, ISMA/AISTA/NFCSF production releases, DFPD balance sheets, ISO quarterly reports, and company investor presentations / earnings-call transcripts / annual reports (BSE/NSE filings). The core deliverable is figure RECONCILIATION, so always capture the estimating body, the exact figure, whether it is gross or net-of-ethanol, and the estimate date. Recency window is strict: sugar figures must be SS 2025-26; rate/crude figures must be 2026 calendar. Measured/official figures outrank aggregator or secondary-press numbers; when only secondary press is available, flag it as unverified. Indian business-press domains (Business Standard, CNBC-TV18, ThePrint, Tribune, Goodreturns) are frequently egress-blocked, so chase the primary source the article cites rather than the article. Where a figure cannot be resolved to a primary source, say so explicitly rather than picking one.

### Inference depth

provenance stated: when no source states X but sourced claims A and B

present an inference as a sourced fact; the citation checker verifies
number-bearing sentences pair-by-pair and an inference dressed as a
quote or finding will be flagged. Audited absences (what the record
should contain but does not) are reportable findings.
