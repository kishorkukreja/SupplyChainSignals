Operator guide

Buying time is useful only if the rest of the system can spend it.

The simulator mirrors the W27 Signal Translation package: metrics prove pressure, the decision queue assigns internal action, and What to Watch stays external.

1

Start with exposed seasonal orders

Use SKU families where a July booking, policy, carton or receiving decision is live.

2

Keep stations separate

Drewry rates, NRF forecasts, USTR dates, CBP refunds and ISM/FAO indexes are not one composite metric.

3

Check proof before speed

Early bookings help service only if supplier evidence, product scope and importer-of-record checks are ready.

4

Make cartons visible

Forest-to-cardboard is this week's lens: carton specs, print approval and pallet patterns can block a frontload.

5

Name the owner

Logistics owns bookings; trade owns proof; procurement owns packaging; finance owns refunds; warehouse owns dock slots.

6

Read recommendation as a meeting agenda

Move, split, hold or pause are prompts for cross-functional decisions, not automated purchase advice.
