## Supply Chain Weather

**Entry proof weather: proof capacity is the front, not the rate table.**

This week's weather map has separate stations for tariff authority, the 24-28 July entry clock, forced-labor proof, freight, import timing, apparel sourcing and input scarcity. They should not be blended into one risk number because each station has a different source, owner and clock.

**Operator translation:** Before the next entry or customer-pricing decision, open one entry proof board: product, origin, HTS, authority, exemption status, in-transit status, supplier proof, broker instruction, landed-cost treatment, customer exposure and named owner. External watch items stay external; internal rows get owners.

**Weather stations to watch**

- **Tariff authority — Red policy front:** watch USTR and Federal Register clarifications on country treatment, product exemptions and annex detail.
- **Entry clock — Cutoff squall:** watch broker instructions, vessel status, warehouse withdrawal timing and entry records around the 24 July effective time and 28 July in-transit relief cutoff.
- **Forced-labor proof — Evidence fog:** watch UFLPA list updates, detention data, sector alerts and supplier proof responsiveness.
- **Freight station — Spot-rate downdraft:** watch Drewry and Freightos lane movements, kept separate from import volume and compliance proof.
- **Import calendar — Pull-forward humidity:** watch whether pull-forward creates entry congestion, inventory overhang or orderly August normalization.
- **Apparel sourcing — Quota-and-proof crosswind:** watch country placement, quota feasibility, cotton-input proof, supplier labor evidence and lead-time tradeoffs.
- **Input scarcity — Commodity watch haze:** watch the 3 August ISM manufacturing release for supplier deliveries, prices and short-supply commodities.
