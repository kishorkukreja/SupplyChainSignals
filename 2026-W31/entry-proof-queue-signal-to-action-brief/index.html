Guide

Use the weekly signal as an entry proof queue, not a tariff dashboard.

What Changed is external fact. Operator Read is interpretation. What To Do This Week is internal action. What To Watch Next stays external-only.

1

Open the row

Each row needs SKU or shipment group, origin, HTS, authority, exemption status, in-transit status, supplier proof, broker instruction, landed-cost treatment and customer exposure.

2

Name the owner

Trade compliance cannot finish without procurement proof, logistics timing, finance treatment and sales language.

3

Keep stations separate

Do not blend freight relief, import pull-forward and tariff proof into one score. Each station has a different source and decision owner.

4

Set the failure mode

The avoidable failure is a broker filing, supplier commitment or customer surcharge that uses a different fact file than finance or compliance.

5

Review in 72 hours

Close rows only when proof, timing, landed cost and customer language point to the same decision.
