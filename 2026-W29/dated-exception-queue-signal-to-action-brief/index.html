Operator guide

Use the weekly signal as a trigger board, not a risk dashboard.

The W29 mechanism is decision congestion. Separate the clocks, name the owner and preserve external watch items as watch items.

1

Start with the clock

Each exception row needs a date or cutoff before it needs a debate: July 22, transcript window, quota window, sailing cutoff, ENS filing or project float.

2

Name the affected file

Convert headlines into proof packets, product-code files, protected booking lists, cargo-data cleanup files or reservation approvals.

3

Assign one primary owner

Customs, legal, procurement, logistics, finance, operations and executives all matter; the board fails if no one owns the first move.

4

Keep watch external

What To Watch is about public triggers. What To Do This Week is about internal action, owners and failure modes.

5

Use ore to equipment as a lens

Steel quotas and electrical-equipment lead times make the exception queue concrete without turning the whole week into an infrastructure-only story.
