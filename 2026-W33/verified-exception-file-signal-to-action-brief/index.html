      Agent Workflow

      Signal Reader → Business Matcher → Decision Coach

      The browser simulates the agent handoff deterministically. The user supplies context; the app supplies operating translation.

      01 Signal Reader

      Separates transshipment enforcement, USTR/Federal Register dates, FreightWaves/Descartes/Xeneta lane cards, AI planning, Rhine water levels, FAO inputs and inspection signals. It rejects a fake composite metric.

      02 Business Matcher

      Maps the live exception to the owner who can unblock it: trade compliance, logistics, planning, procurement, quality or finance.

      03 Decision Coach

      Turns that match into a 72-hour file: source, owner, proof artifact, deadline, approval and failure mode.

      Guide

      How to use W33 without doing the signal translation yourself.

      1. Start with the row, not the news.

      Name the SKU, supplier, shipment, route, entry, storage site or equipment slot that could fail review.

      2. Keep What Changed external.

      Public facts prove pressure: enforcement checks, import/rate divergence, AI adoption, Rhine constraints and food inspections.

      3. Turn Operator Read into ownership.

      Every row gets one primary owner, one proof artifact, one approval path and one failure mode.

      4. Keep What To Watch external.

      Watch CBP/USTR updates, rate/import calendars, Rhine water, AI planning controls, food-input categories and inspection enforcement.
