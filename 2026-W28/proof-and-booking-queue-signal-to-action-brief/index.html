Operator guide

Use the signal without overcomplicating it.

The W28 mechanism is simple: July front-loading buys time, but it also creates proof, cost and allocation work.

1

Keep facts and interpretation separate

TEU, USTR, WCI, diesel and ISM are source facts. The operator read is the decision queue they create.

2

Name the lane

Every issue should land in booking, proof, cost or strategic inputs. If it does not change a decision, leave it on watch.

3

Do not make a blended score

Different signals have different owners and clocks. A single risk number hides who needs to act.

4

Use field to fork as a case lens

For food and ingredients, check fuel sensitivity, cold-chain capacity, supplier documentation and margin triggers.

5

Separate watch from action

What to Watch is external. What To Do This Week is internal. Do not confuse monitoring with ownership.
